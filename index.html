We grew fruit for the world for 25 years.
Then we decided to pour it into a glass for you.

Back in 1996, Mr. G.V.K. Naidu started Sam Agri with a simple goal: to grow the best fruit in INDIA and deliver it fresh to the world. For 25 years, we sent residue-free pomegranates to Swiss supermarkets and fresh coconuts to the UAE. The finest fruit stayed right here in Nashik—so in 2022, we transformed it into extraordinary, natural Fruit & Flower Wines.
