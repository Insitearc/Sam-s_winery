Mixology & Craft

Signature Cocktail Recipe

Curated specifically to highlight the vibrant, sweet-tart fruit notes of Scarlet Silk

Prep: 6 mins Easy Serves: 1 Glass

Strawberry Basil Breeze

A bright, aromatic mix that highlights the sweet, freshly-crushed berry character of Strawberry Wine, elevated with fresh green basil leaves and a sparkling finish.

Ingredients

  • 120ml Sam's Strawberry Wine
  • 3-4 Fresh Basil leaves
  • 15ml Fresh Lemon juice
  • 3 Sliced fresh Strawberries
  • Sparkling water or Prosecco to top

Instructions

  1. Place basil leaves and sliced strawberries at the bottom of a large glass.
  2. Gently muddle with a spoon to release essential oils and juice.
  3. Fill glass halfway with cubed ice, pour in lemon juice and Sam's Strawberry Wine.
  4. Top with sparkling water or Prosecco and garnish with fresh basil.
Sommelier Tip: Muddle sweet Thai basil or holy basil for a delicious herbal aroma that pairs magnificently with ripe strawberry sweetness.
